Self-paced **tracks of tiny puzzles** — 5 to 10 minutes each — for practicing ways of thinking:

- **Functional & parallel thinking** — pure functions, recursion, higher-order functions, algebraic
  data types, folds, and the `map`/`reduce`/`scan` reasoning that lets a runtime spread work across
  many cores without you ever writing a lock.
- **Bayesian statistics** — probability as plausibility: conditioning, Bayes' theorem, priors and
  posteriors, and calibration training so you learn how much to trust your own estimates.

Each lesson stands on its own and re-explains the terms it uses, so you can drop in anywhere. When a
lesson builds on an earlier one, it links back. Solve the puzzle, then read the solution page for the
full explanation. Your spot in each track is remembered so you can pick up on any device.
